Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how the game Blade at the Gate (the “Game”), made by DEVELOPER_NAME, handles your information. In short: the Game does not collect personal data.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no third-party tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (level, coins, upgrades, language, sound options and the hero name you choose) are saved only on your device. They never leave it. Deleting the Game deletes this data.

Internet connection

When the Game starts it downloads a small file to check whether a newer version is required. No personal information or device identifiers are sent with this request. Like any website, the server that hosts this file may briefly keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of the request). We do not use them to identify you. The Game works offline.

Permissions

On Android the Game uses vibration (for game feedback) and internet access (for the version check only).

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. Since the Game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
