Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to petrosyankhachatur1@gmail.com. Please mention your phone model.

FAQ

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the game or clearing its data deletes your progress.

How do I move the hero?

Put your finger anywhere on the battlefield and slide it: the hero follows the movement of your finger.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings from the main menu and choose a language.

The game asks me to update.

A newer version is required. Tap “Update” to open the store page, install the update, and your progress will be kept.
